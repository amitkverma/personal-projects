# Tapkit

## Product Overview

Tapkit is a Mac application that enables AI agents to control iPhones by seeing, tapping, and typing across apps. The tool transforms iPhones into programmable devices accessible via API, allowing agents to perform any action a human could do on a phone.

## What It Does

The platform provides programmatic control of physical iPhones through Apple's accessibility features. As stated on the site, "Anything you can do on your phone, your agent can do too."

### Key Capabilities

- Downloads and installs apps
- Sends iMessages
- Handles 2FA and verification processes
- Accesses iCloud services
- Makes payments via Apple Wallet
- Locks and unlocks devices
- Executes any app-based workflow

## Target Audience

Businesses and developers who need AI agents to interact with mobile apps programmatically, including personal assistance, account operations, and app development workflows.

## Pricing & Business Model

Subscription-based, per-phone pricing:
- **One iPhone**: $49/month
- **Five iPhones**: $245/month
- **Fleet Plans**: Custom pricing at $49 per phone/month with setup and onboarding

Users bring their own iPhones; no special hardware required. Monthly billing with cancellation flexibility.

## How It Works

1. Download the Mac app
2. Connect your AI agent via MCP server, REST API, or web interface
3. Agents receive screenshots, send commands (tap, swipe, type) via API
4. Actions execute on the physical iPhone

Integrates with Claude Code, Cursor, custom AI agents; offers no-code and developer options.
