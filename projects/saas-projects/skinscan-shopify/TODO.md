# TODO — SkinScan for Shopify

For Indian D2C skincare brands on Shopify or WooCommerce (Foxtale, Minimalist-sized and smaller) that can't afford Haut.ai's enterprise sales process. Right now shoppers guess which serum suits them, and conversion and returns suffer. v1 is a one-click store widget: a selfie scan tuned for Indian skin (tanning, pigmentation, oiliness, acne marks), a routine built from the brand's own catalogue, and one-tap add to cart. Billed per scan in INR.
