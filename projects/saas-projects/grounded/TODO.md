# TODO — Grounded

For solo and small-practice therapists. The therapist side covers AI session notes, treatment plans and progress tracking. A companion app gives clients CBT/DBT homework to check off and logs journaling, walks and steps (HealthKit/Google Fit), sleep and mood between sessions. The therapist gets an adherence dashboard and a pre-session brief on what actually happened that week, so therapy runs on real data instead of recall. V1: record a session and get a SOAP/DAP note; assign homework that the client checks off in the app; see adherence and activity trends on one timeline per client.
