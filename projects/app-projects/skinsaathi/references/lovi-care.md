# Lóvi — Smart Skin Care

"Science-backed AI Cosmetologist you can trust."

Lóvi is an iOS application functioning as a personalized skincare assistant powered by AI and backed by medical professionals. It combines facial analysis, product evaluation, and expert guidance to help users develop customized skincare routines.

## Key features

### Face scanning
- Analyzes 11+ skin conditions including acne, wrinkles, pigmentation, scars, and redness
- Tracks changes over time with monthly progress visualization
- Trained by a medical board of dermatologists and cosmetic chemists

### Product scanner (Lóvi Fit Score)
- Evaluates cosmetics from 19+ brands including CeraVe, Paula's Choice, and The Ordinary
- Assesses ingredient safety against international standards (FDA, European Chemical Agency, TGA)
- Provides compatibility ratings with the user's skin type and existing routine
- Claims "100% independent" with no brand affiliation

### AI cosmetologist assistant
- Answers skincare questions 24/7
- Offers personalized routine recommendations
- Suggests product alternatives based on ingredient analysis
- Provides UV index alerts and seasonal guidance

### Routine building
- Creates customized skincare regimens
- Tracks routine effectiveness over time

## Target audience
Beginners and skincare enthusiasts seeking affordable, science-based product recommendations and skin health monitoring.
