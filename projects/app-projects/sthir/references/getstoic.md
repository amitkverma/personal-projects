# Beat Stress in 120 Seconds — AI-Powered Journal | Stoic

## What the Product Does

Stoic is an AI-powered journaling application designed to help users manage stress, anxiety, and mental health through daily reflective writing. The app combines guided prompts with artificial intelligence to personalize the journaling experience within 120 seconds per session.

## Key Features

- **Guided Journaling Prompts**: Curated daily prompts from therapists and experts to deepen personal reflection
- **AI Personalization**: Machine learning customizes journal suggestions based on user responses
- **Mood & Trends Tracking**: Monitor emotional patterns and progress over time
- **Daily Reflections**: Morning check-ins and evening wrap-ups with personalized guidance
- **Mindful Exercises**: Built-in meditation and breathing practices
- **Habit Building Tools**: Streak trackers, smart reminders, and achievement badges
- **Inspiration Library**: Access to quotes, affirmations, and themed events
- **Cross-Device Syncing**: Seamless synchronization across Apple devices and web
- **Complete Privacy**: Journals stored locally on devices with secure backup; no third-party access

## Who It Serves

Individuals seeking stress management, anxiety relief, mental health support, and personal growth through structured reflection, from those managing depression and anxiety to people building positive daily habits.
