# Wispr Flow (UX reference)

Voice-to-text dictation AI that turns speech into polished writing in any app. Removes filler words, handles mid-sentence corrections, adds punctuation and formatting; claims "4x faster" than typing.

## Who it serves
Professionals at Microsoft, Amazon, Notion, Klarna, Groupon, Vercel, Mercury. Marketed to leaders, developers, creators, support teams, students, lawyers, sales, and accessibility users.

## Key features
- 100+ languages with auto-detection
- Personal vocabulary learning (names, jargon)
- Text snippets that expand into frequent content
- Tone adapts per app (formal/casual)
- Works everywhere with no plugins: Gmail, Slack, iMessage, Notion, terminals, editors, ChatGPT, Claude
- Notetaker: meeting transcription, speaker names, topic summaries, action items

## UX / interface
- Floating **Flow bar** with one-click language switching
- Overlay works from any app's text input, no setup
- Animations show real-time cleanup as you speak; the focus is on zero friction

## Pricing
- Free: 2,000 words/week
- Flow Pro: unlimited
- Flow Pro for Teams: central billing, admin portal
- Enterprise: SSO, audit logs, bulk pricing
