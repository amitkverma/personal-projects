# Typeless (UX reference)

"Speak naturally, and Typeless will turn your words into polished messages, emails, and documents", in real time; claims 4x faster than typing and about a day a week saved.

## Who it serves
Professionals (educators, engineers, physicians, founders) who want consistent voice input across devices.

## Key features
- Removes fillers and repetitions; self-corrects mid-sentence changes
- Auto-formats lists and steps; adapts tone per app
- Learns the user's voice and phrasing; personal dictionary
- 100+ languages with real-time detection; translate as you speak
- Edit selected text by voice; summarize/explain read-only pages and documents
- Quick answers and cross-service search

## UX / interface
- Works across 50+ apps (Notion, Slack, VS Code, Gmail)
- System-wide voice activation on macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android
- Homepage gives no hotkey or animation specifics

## Privacy
- Zero cloud retention for voice processing
- On-device, encrypted history
- ISO 27001 and HIPAA compliant; data not used for training
