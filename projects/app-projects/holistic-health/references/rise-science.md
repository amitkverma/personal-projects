# RISE — Sleep and energy tracker

Helps users improve sleep and daily performance by managing sleep debt and energy.

## Sleep Debt
"The only sleep score that matters is sleep debt." Tracks how much sleep your body needs and helps repay the deficit.

## Circadian Rhythm
Predicts daily energy patterns and guides the sleep-wake cycle.

## Energy
Daily insights: grogginess duration, peak focus times, wind-down schedule.

## Users and model
Productivity-focused professionals, people with low energy or poor sleep, wearable owners (Apple Health, Oura, Fitbit). iOS and Android, free trial then premium; B2B for sales teams. "80% of RISE users feel the benefits within 5 days."
