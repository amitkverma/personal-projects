# The Outsiders (Gentler Stories) — Training readiness app

Training readiness and performance analysis for endurance athletes (runners, cyclists) on Apple Watch and iPhone; guides daily training decisions and prevents overtraining.

## Features
- **Training Readiness Score**: push hard, dial back, or rest — from training load, sleep and body metrics
- **Training Load Ratio**: recent effort vs long-term fitness base
- **Body Metrics**: sleeping HR, HRV, wrist temperature, respiratory rate, blood oxygen
- **Sleep Analysis**: duration, stages, consistency and their effect on training capacity
- **Advanced Metrics**: cardio fitness, endurance fitness, cycling FTP, intensity distribution, zones
- **Custom goals**: distance, duration, elevation, load, sessions, HR/power targets
- **Integrations**: Apple Health import; Garmin Connect

## Data displayed
Session summaries (duration, distance, elevation, speed, power, HR), zone breakdowns, intervals/splits, Relative Intensity, Variability Index, route maps with terrain.

## Model
Free trial then paid app purchase; no subscription; data stays on device, no cloud account.
