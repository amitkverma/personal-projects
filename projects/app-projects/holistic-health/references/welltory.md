# Welltory — Health intelligence platform

Analyzes 100+ biomarkers to show what drains your energy and gives personalized recovery, sleep and health guidance.

> "See what's draining your energy — and stop it before it drains your day."

## HRV analysis
- Measurements through the day with proprietary algorithms
- Compared against personal baseline, not generic norms
- Backed by 25,000+ peer-reviewed studies

## Insights
- Blood pressure monitoring with change alerts
- Stress impact and energy drain
- Real-time nervous system snapshots
- Sleep quality, workout effectiveness, recovery ("learn what restores energy")

## Target users
Chronic fatigue and complex conditions (fibromyalgia, lupus), energy-limiting conditions needing pacing, "normal" labs despite symptoms, burnout.
> "If you wake up tired... You're who we built our app for."

## Model
Freemium; core free forever; revenue from premium subscriptions, not data sales. Up to 48% discount promo; premium free in war/crisis regions. "Welltory does not sell personal health data."
