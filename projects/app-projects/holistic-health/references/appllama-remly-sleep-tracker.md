# Sleep Tracker & Sound by Remly (Appllama listing)

## Summary

This is Appllama's market-data page for Remly, an iOS sleep tracker from DEEP FLOW SOFTWARE SERVICES, a studio in the UAE. It launched in September 2024 and ranks #105 in US Health & Fitness. It is rated 4.8★ from 19.2K ratings and had about 80K downloads last month. It costs $4.99/week or $24.99/year; Appllama's revenue estimate is behind its Pro paywall. The page lists a 19-screen onboarding, one paywall, and 13 in-app flows. Those flows include sleep tracking, a sleep log, a 5-screen heart-rate flow, breathwork, sounds and mixing, a sleep alarm, "Sleep Help", a "Dream Streak" and an AI "Dreambot".

## Why it matters for this project

- **Sleep pillar (competitive landscape):** a young, fast-growing standalone sleep app. Holistic Health's Sleep pillar has to match it in tracking, a sleep log and sleep sounds, or explain why users would leave it.
- **Heart pillar (competitive landscape):** Remly already adds heart rate (5 screens) and breathwork (4 screens) to a sleep app. Single-pillar apps are spreading into the next pillar, which weakens the "one app instead of five" pitch for the **Pentad** angle unless the cross-pillar AI guide is clearly better.
- **AI guide (PRD):** Dreambot is a chat assistant tied to sleep alone. It's the baseline Holistic Health's AI guide must beat by reasoning across sleep, HRV, mood and biomarkers rather than sleep only.
- **Pricing (lean canvas):** $4.99/week and $24.99/year for sleep alone sets the floor. An all-in-one that replaces Remly plus Welltory, Stoic and others can charge more, but has to sit visibly below their combined price.
- **Onboarding and paywall (PRD):** the 19-screen onboarding that ends in a paywall is the current pattern for subscription health apps, and a reference length for Holistic Health's own onboarding.
