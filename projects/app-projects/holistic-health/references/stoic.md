# Stoic — AI-powered journaling app

Guided daily reflections to reduce stress and improve mental health, about two minutes per session, with AI-personalized prompts and therapeutic guidance.

## Journaling and mood
- Personalized AI-guided prompts
- Morning and evening check-ins
- Mood and trend tracking over time
- Themed weekly journaling events

## Mental health support
- Expressive writing to "Fight Stress & Anxiety"
- Meditation and breathing exercises
- Affirmations and Stoic quotes
- Habits with streaks and badges

## Privacy
Journals stored locally; encrypted cloud backup; sync across Apple devices and web.

## Users and model
People seeking accessible mental health support; stress, anxiety, depression. Free on iOS, macOS, Android, web; freemium implied. 3M+ users, YC S19, App of the Day in 100+ countries, 4.8 stars.
