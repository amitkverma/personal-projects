# Bevel — The Connected Health Coach

AI-powered health coaching app that integrates wearable data and bloodwork into one platform, turning fragmented health signals into actionable insights.

## Features and metrics
- **Strain**: daily effort and exertion
- **Sleep**: stage tracking (REM, core, deep)
- **Recovery**: readiness from HRV and heart rate
- **Nutrition**: food logging with macro/micronutrient breakdowns
- **Health Records**: lab results and clinical notes
- **Biological Age**: single score showing aging trajectory
- **Cycle Tracking**: menstrual cycle awareness for training
- **Strength Training**: lift logging and progress
- **Other**: journal, caffeine, smart alarm, water, activity status

## Integrations
Apple Watch, Oura, Garmin, Amazfit, Google Health, plus 30+ lab partners (LabCorp, Quest, Lifelabs, etc.).

## Users and model
Health-conscious people, fitness enthusiasts, people monitoring conditions. "Get started with Bevel for free today" — freemium; premium tiers not shown on homepage. 2.5M+ members, 4.8/5 from 49,100+ reviews.
