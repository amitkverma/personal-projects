# TODO — Holistic Health (working name)

A global health tracker that pulls five areas into one app, with an AI guide that reads across all of them: **Dashboard** (Bevel / The Outsiders-style strain, recovery and readiness), **Mind** (Stoic-style journaling and mood), **Heart** (Welltory-style HRV and stress), **Sleep** (Rise-style sleep debt and circadian energy), and **Biology** (DEXA scans and blood biomarkers).

**Angle still open.** Candidates from `/idea` (pick one before grilling):
- **Pentad**: one app for the quantified-self user juggling Bevel, Stoic, Welltory, Rise and a lab portal; AI coach reasons across all five pillars.
- **Biomarker Coach**: lead with Biology; upload DEXA/blood PDFs, get trends, explanations and a next-steps plan tied to wearable data.
- **Energy OS**: for burned-out professionals; sleep debt + HRV stress + 2-minute check-ins become one daily energy budget.
- **Private Health Vault**: privacy-first and on-device, encrypted sync, one-time purchase.
