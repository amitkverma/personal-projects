# Ahead: The Duolingo for your emotional intelligence

Ahead is a mobile app on the App Store and Google Play. It calls itself "The Duolingo for your emotional intelligence" and an AI "pocket therapist", built by behaviour scientists trained at Oxford, Cambridge and Harvard. Company: Ahead Solutions GmbH, Berlin.

## What it does

A daily coach for managing emotions. It helps users understand why they feel what they feel, recognise feelings "before they take control", and track progress over time.

## Target users

Individuals who want to build emotional intelligence and manage their emotions. A "For Business" section targets organisations as a secondary audience.

## Core features

- Personalised journey designed by behaviour scientists
- Available "24/7" as a pocket coach
- Insight into behavioural patterns
- 100+ "science proven techniques"; users choose what works for them
- Daily progress tracking: celebrate wins, learn from setbacks
- Daily lessons with activities and suggested further resources (per testimonials)

## Method

Emotional intelligence and "science proven techniques"; no specific framework is named, and CBT is not mentioned.
