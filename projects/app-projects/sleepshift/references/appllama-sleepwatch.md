# SleepWatch - Top Sleep Tracker (Appllama listing)

## Summary

This is Appllama's market-data page for SleepWatch, an iOS sleep tracker from Bodymatter, Inc. in the US. Released in August 2016, it is the oldest app in the set and has by far the most ratings (328K, at 4.7★). It ranks #179 in US Health & Fitness and had about 30K downloads last month. ## Why it matters for this project

- **Competitive landscape:** the established player. Ten years of releases and 328K ratings, yet only 30K downloads a month against Remly's 80K and Leap Health's 90K. It suggests newer apps with heavier onboarding funnels are taking new users from older leaders.
- **Feature scope (PRD):** Tag Insights is the pattern for connecting behaviour to sleep: users tag a night with things like caffeine or a late meal and see how each affects their sleep. SleepShift could apply it to shift type, nap timing, caffeine cutoff and light, which is the evidence for its daily plan.
- **Sleep reports (PRD):** 8 sleep-report screens make it the most report-heavy app in the set. It's a reference for how much detail to show after each night.
- **Pricing (lean canvas):** $3.99/month and $19.99/year is a monthly-first model with no weekly plan, the lowest monthly price in the set.
