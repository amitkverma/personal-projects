# RISE Sleep Tracker

## Product Overview

RISE is a sleep and energy tracking app designed to help users improve their sleep quality and daily energy levels. The platform enables people to "reach your potential" by optimizing sleep and managing energy throughout the day.

## What the Product Does

The app tracks sleep debt ("how much sleep you owe your body") and provides users with personalized energy schedules based on their circadian rhythm. Users receive daily recommendations including grogginess duration, optimal focus times, and wind-down schedules.

## Key Features

**Sleep Debt Tracking**
- Monitors accumulated sleep debt and helps users pay it back
- Described as "the only sleep score that matters"

**Circadian Rhythm Management**
- Predicts and helps users control their daily energy schedule
- Provides personalized timing for activities

**Science-Based Habits**
- Offers 16 science-based habit nudges timed for optimal effectiveness

**Multi-Day Functionality**
- Day 1: Analyzes historical phone data for sleep needs and debt
- Daily: Provides energy forecasts and recommendations
- Features wearable integration and continuous optimization

## Platforms & Integrations

- Available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play Store)
- Works with Apple Health, Oura, and Fitbit wearables
- Does not require a wearable device to function
