# Aumio: Sleep Sounds & Stories (Appllama listing)

## Summary

This is Appllama's market-data page for Aumio, a sleep-sounds and bedtime-stories app from Aumio GmbH in Germany. It is listed under Education, not Health & Fitness, and opens with a mascot splash screen. ## Why it matters for this project

- **Competitive landscape:** a different kind of competitor. Aumio sells audio content (stories and sounds) rather than tracking, so it shows the content side of the sleep market, not SleepShift's planning angle.
- **Pricing (lean canvas):** $44.99/year is the top of the range in the set (against $9.99–$24.99 for the trackers). It suggests that original audio content sells for more than tracking. That is relevant only if SleepShift adds wind-down audio before daytime sleep.
- **Demand signal:** 1K downloads a month on this storefront suggests that a content-led sleep app without a tracking hook struggles here. It is weak evidence for building SleepShift around content.
