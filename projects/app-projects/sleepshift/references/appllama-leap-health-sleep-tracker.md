# Sleep Tracker: Recorder, Sound by Leap Health (Appllama listing)

## Summary

This is Appllama's market-data page for "Sleep Tracker: Recorder, Sound", an iOS sleep app from Leap Health in Hong Kong. It launched in September 2021 and had about 90K downloads last month, the most of the sleep trackers saved here so far. It is rated 4.8★ from 7.9K ratings. It costs $4.99/month or $9.99/year; the revenue estimate is behind Appllama's Pro paywall. The page lists 68 screens, including a 30-screen onboarding and one paywall. Beyond tracking, sounds and alarms, it has stretching, breathing, body-scan, sleep-journal and CBT-I flows (a CBT-I program plus CBT-I lessons). Only the welcome screens can be seen without Pro.

## Why it matters for this project

- **Competitive landscape:** the highest-download tracker in the set, and one that goes past measuring sleep into coaching. A CBT-I program (cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia, the standard non-drug treatment) is the closest thing here to SleepShift's daily plan, so it is the competitor to study for the coaching layer.
- **Pricing (lean canvas):** $9.99/year is the cheapest yearly plan in the set. It suggests that sleep tracking on its own is getting cheap and that SleepShift's price has to rest on the shift-roster plan.
- **Onboarding and paywall (PRD):** a 30-screen onboarding is the longest in the set. Leap Health runs many health apps, so this length is probably tested and is a data point when deciding SleepShift's onboarding length.
- **Feature scope (PRD):** short CBT-I lessons plus a sleep journal is a content format SleepShift could adapt for shift-work guidance (anchor sleep, naps, light).
