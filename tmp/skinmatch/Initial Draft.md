# SkinMatch — The Idea

*A simple guide to what we're building and why.*

**Date:** 14 June 2026

---

Inspiration : https://dribbble.com/shots/25874930-AI-Health-Copilot-From-User-Story-to-Human-Centered-Experience

## What is it?

SkinMatch is an app that helps people find the right skincare products based on **what's actually inside them** — not just the brand name or the marketing on the bottle.

Most people buy skincare blind. The labels are full of confusing ingredient names, the claims on the front are vague, and a fancy expensive cream is often almost identical to a much cheaper one. SkinMatch cuts through all of that.

---

## The problem we're solving

- **Ingredient lists are confusing.** People can't tell what's good for them or what to avoid.  
- **Expensive doesn't always mean better.** Two products can be nearly the same inside but cost very differently.  
- **Sensitive skin is risky.** People react badly to certain ingredients and have no easy way to spot and avoid them.  
- **No clear starting point.** Beginners don't know which products to use, in what order, or how.

---

## What people can do with it

### 1\. Search by what's inside a product

Look up products by their ingredients — find ones that *contain* something helpful, or *avoid* something that doesn't agree with you.

### 2\. Search by your skin concern

Pick what you're trying to fix — acne, dark spots, dryness, fine lines, dullness — and see products that genuinely target it.

### 3\. Filter by your sensitivities

Tell the app what your skin reacts to (for example fragrance or alcohol), and it warns you or hides products that contain those things.

### 4\. Find a cheaper alternative

Love an expensive product but not the price? The app finds more affordable products with a very similar makeup, and shows you how much you'd save.

### 5\. Learn about ingredients in plain English

Every ingredient has a simple page explaining what it is, what it does for your skin, how to use it, and what *not* to combine it with.

### 6\. Get a personal routine from the AI

Tell the app about your skin, your concerns, and your budget. The AI builds a step-by-step morning and evening routine to help with your skin issues — and explains *why* each step is there. You can ask it to adjust: "make it cheaper", "remove fragrance", "add something for dark spots".

---

## Where the products come from

The app gathers product listings and prices from **multiple shopping websites**, so people can compare options and prices in one place instead of hunting around. We don't sell products directly — we point people to where they can buy.

---

## Who it's for

- **The careful shopper** who wants to know exactly what they're putting on their skin.  
- **The budget shopper** who wants the same results for less money.  
- **The sensitive-skin person** who needs to avoid certain ingredients.  
- **The beginner** who has no idea where to start and wants to be guided.  
- **The skincare lover** who enjoys comparing ingredients and hunting for better deals.

---

## What makes it different

- It's built around **ingredients and results**, not brands and hype.  
- It actively helps people **save money** by finding cheaper look-alikes.  
- It keeps **sensitive skin safe** by flagging problem ingredients.  
- It gives a **personal, guided routine** instead of leaving people to guess.

---

## Important note

SkinMatch is a helpful guide, not a doctor. For serious skin conditions, people should always see a dermatologist. The app will make this clear.

---

## How we'd roll it out

- **Step 1 — Start simple:** Search products and ingredients, explain ingredients in plain language, and let people flag what they're sensitive to.  
- **Step 2 — Save money:** Add the "cheaper alternative" finder and price comparison across shops.  
- **Step 3 — Get personal:** Launch the AI routine builder.  
- **Step 4 — Grow:** Add accounts, saved routines, and more shops and regions.
